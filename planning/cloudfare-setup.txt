**3. Configure the public hostname**
Still in the tunnel setup, under **Public Hostnames** add:
- Subdomain: `plaid-callback`
- Domain: `forbell.com`
- Service: `HTTP` → `localhost:3004`
- Path (optional but recommended): `/oauth/callback`

This creates `https://plaid-callback.forbell.com/oauth/callback` pointing to erebor.
